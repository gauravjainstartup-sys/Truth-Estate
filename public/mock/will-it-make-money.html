Modeled, not a promise, recalculated for 21 Aug 2026. The model’s structure is agreed; the magnitudes are uncalibrated placeholders pending a backtest against realised appreciation. It models the property’s price return and rent only — registration & stamp duty (~6–7%), interiors, and ongoing maintenance & property tax are not yet included. XIRR is the annualised return on the actual cash flows: staged construction outflow, rent once ready, sale at exit.
